This is the build for the Slay the Spire 2 beta branch (Steam > Properties > Betas). On the default branch? Use the main CouchSpire item instead: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810018612
Subscribe to only one of the two.

Couch co-op on one screen, no networking required. Each controller drives its own character in a
local multi-character run: press a button on your controller to take over whenever your teammate
isn't mid-action.

- Per-controller seats: plug in a second controller and press a button to claim your character.
- Simultaneous play in combat: the teammate who isn't driving can still play cards, use potions,
  and end their turn without taking the screen.
- Full per-character state: decks, gold, potions, relics, and choices are all tracked separately
  instead of mirrored or shared.
- Rewards, shops, rest sites, treasure, and events are all handled per player.
- Works on Steam Deck / Bazzite Game Mode.

Based on Local Multi-Control (DualRoleAdventure) - do not enable both mods at once.

Source, issue tracker, and full setup docs: https://github.com/bcjarrett/couchspire
